Draco huffs, lips pressing into a pout. “I can’t believe it took me nesting in your bed for you to finally make a move on me,” he grumbles, sounding thoroughly put out.
Theo raises a brow, amusement dancing in his eyes. “Hm? You wanted me to make a move?” He can’t help but tease—Draco looks too damn adorable sulking like this.
Draco’s head snaps up, eyes flashing with exasperation. “Why do you think I went out of my way to tease you and annoy you all the time? Do you honestly believe I’d waste so much time and energy on you if I didn’t like you? Merlin, you’re dense—” He cuts himself off abruptly, his voice faltering before something more fragile slips through. “Did you really… not have feelings for me at all before this, Theo?”
It’s raw, unmistakably vulnerable. Like he’s just handed Theo a loaded gun and is waiting to see if he’ll pull the trigger.
Theo blinks, his expression unreadable at first—then, to Draco’s absolute horror, his lips twitch.
“Why do you think I put up with you all this time, huh?” he drawls lazily. “I thought you were just being annoying. How was I supposed to know the most sought-after omega in school was flirting with me—and doing a truly pathetic job at it?”
Draco lets out an indignant squawk and immediately pinches Theo’s side, making him yelp before bursting into laughter.
Before Draco can strike again, Theo catches his wrist, smirking as he cups Draco’s cheek and pulls him closer. His voice drops, turning softer, steadier.
“Well,” he murmurs, thumb brushing slow, lazy circles over Draco’s warm skin. “You can stop all that coy flirting now… my omega.”
Draco wants to argue, to huff and pout, but the words unravel him. Just like that, his resolve is gone, leaving him utterly undone. Theo’s voice, his touch, the weight of those two words—it’s enough to melt him from the inside out. And the way Theo looks at him, like he’s something precious, something to be claimed but never caged, tells Draco everything he needs to know.
Theo is just as smitten.
The space between them vanishes as Theo leans in, slow and deliberate, his breath fanning against Draco’s lips.
“My omega,” he murmurs again, the words barely more than a whisper. A promise.
And then their lips meet in a kiss—slow, warm, and unhurried. Theo doesn’t rush, doesn’t demand, just claims him in a way that makes Draco’s heart stutter.
When they part, Theo lingers, his forehead resting against Draco’s, his lips brushing over his in soft, fleeting touches.
“Mine,” he whispers again, steady and sure.
And Draco? Draco doesn’t fight it. He just leans in and lets himself be Theo’s.
